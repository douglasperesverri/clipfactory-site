ClipFactory

A private desktop tool that turns long videos into vertical Shorts and schedules them on its owner's own YouTube channels.

ClipFactory uses YouTube API Services

ClipFactory connects to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API. It is used only by its owner, Douglas Peres Verri, to publish Shorts on up to ten YouTube channels that the owner owns and manages. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

By using ClipFactory you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Please also read our Privacy Policy, our Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

Edits

Finds the best moments of a long video, reframes them to 9:16 and adds captions, all on the owner's computer.

Schedules

Uploads each Short to the owner's channel as private with a scheduled publish time, every few hours per channel.

Checks

Reads the status of its own uploads to confirm each Short went public at the right time.

Google account access

The owner connects each channel with Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0). ClipFactory asks for two permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload each approved Short to the channel as private with a scheduled publish time.
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube): to set the title, description, tags and publish time of the tool's own uploads, read their status and the publish times already scheduled on the channel (so new Shorts do not clash with them), read the channel's ID and title, and delete a draft left behind by a failed upload.

ClipFactory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See the Privacy Policy for details.
